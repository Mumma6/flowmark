---
title: Account and data controls
description: Manage sessions, verified email changes, exports and deletion safely.
---

Account controls remain available before SDK setup is complete. Open **Account**,
**Account security** or **Account data & privacy** from setup; Studio also exposes
account controls and **Settings → Data & privacy**.

## Sign-in and credentials

Your email/password signs you into Studio. Optional email verification proves
mailbox ownership; it is not generally required to create a project. Invitation
acceptance and verified email changes have their own proof requirements.
Use **Forgot password?** for recovery. A verified email change requires your
password and proof of both the old and new addresses; follow the displayed
cancellation/deadline controls rather than changing email through another route.

Account security lists browser sessions and supports password-confirmed sign-out.
Signing out a browser session does not revoke agent connections or project keys.
Manage agent connections in **Connect**, and ingestion/read keys in Settings.
Keep write keys on the server; their full values are shown only when issued.

## Download your data

Under **Download your data**, choose the scope, review the local-file warning and
confirm your password:

- **My account personal data:** your profile, membership and safe account/connection
  metadata and private selections. Membership does not automatically include all
  project telemetry in this export.
- **Selected project data:** requires the current owner and includes that project's
  evidence/configuration and the requester's private selections, not other users'
  private selections.

Download every numbered NDJSON part through the final `complete: true` marker.
There is no emailed export or background artifact. Requests expire after fifteen
minutes and remain bound to current session/ownership checks. Retry the current
part or resume as the UI permits; do not assume one part is the full export.

Credential secrets, password hashes and session tokens are excluded. Reported
application payloads can still contain sensitive information. Store downloaded
files accordingly. A portable presentation or clipboard copy is not this export.

## Delete evidence or an account

Review the exact scope and counts, confirm the project name where requested and
verify your password. Download needed evidence before deletion. Destructive
controls require sole ownership; shared projects are blocked. Ownership transfer
is not provided as a workaround.

Confirmed erasure invalidates project keys and fences ordinary access while
bounded cleanup proceeds. A failed batch is not completed deletion. Use **Resume
cleanup** for the same operation; do not repeat an unrelated delete. Closing the
page or pausing stops continuation, not previously committed deletion.

Backups/provider records and previously downloaded copies have separate lifetimes.
These controls do not promise instant removal from every system. Consult
[security and privacy](/docs/security-privacy) for telemetry boundaries and contact
the maintainer privately for unresolved rights/account questions.
