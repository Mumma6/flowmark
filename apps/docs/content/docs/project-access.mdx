---
title: Projects and access
description: Understand ownership, diagnostic viewers, business readers and published evidence.
---

A project scopes evidence and access. In **Settings → Project access**, the fixed
owner can invite a colleague by email, choose a role and confirm the scope with
their password. Invitation acceptance requires the intended verified email.
A shared investigation link is not a membership invitation.

| Role              | Access                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner             | Manages the project, keys, invitations and business publication.                                                          |
| Diagnostic viewer | Reads full project diagnostics, including reported properties, errors, results and diagnostic downloads.                  |
| Business reader   | Reads project-wide activity metadata and explicitly published business results/references, with raw diagnostics withheld. |

Business access is **project-wide**, not customer-specific. Searching a customer
reference filters evidence; it does not create customer-level permissions. Only
invite someone who should see the project's permitted metadata.

## Publish business evidence

Under **Business evidence publication**, the owner selects declared result paths
for an exact immutable workflow revision and reference keys for particular
workflows. Results and references start unpublished. A new result revision needs
its own publication selection.

Published labels, units and category vocabularies explain application-reported
values. Missing, invalid or unpublished values stay unavailable. Publication does
not establish that the application reported the truth or that a completed run
succeeded for every record.

Business readers cannot use raw events, properties, errors or technical filters
to bypass publication. Portable views and agent reads retain their applicable
authority and projection rules. See [MCP](/docs/mcp) and
[shared investigations](/docs/investigations).

## Remove access

The owner manages membership in Project access. Subsequent server reads recheck
authority. Removal does not recall screenshots or previously downloaded files.
Browser sessions, agent grants and project keys have separate lifecycles; use
[account and data controls](/docs/account-data) for the distinction.

There is one fixed owner. Ownership transfer is not implemented. Shared projects
are blocked from destructive lifecycle actions that require sole ownership;
removing access is not an ownership-transfer mechanism.
